About

Small agency. Big on strategy.

Laiton Digital is a remote social media and Meta ads agency built on one idea: marketing should bring in customers.

Our story

Built by a social media marketer, for businesses that want inquiries.

Laiton Digital is led by a social media marketer with 12 years of experience across content, campaigns and community. We kept seeing the same problem: businesses posting constantly and getting nothing back, because the posts had no plan, no offer and no next step.

So we built an agency around the fix: a clear plan every month, content designed to convert, ads that are tracked and honest reporting. Remote and flexible, so we can work with businesses in any industry, in the UK and beyond.
